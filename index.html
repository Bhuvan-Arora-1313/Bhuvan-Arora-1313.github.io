About

I build environments and benchmarks that test whether language-model agents can handle long, messy, real-world jobs, and I use them to train better agents. Most of my work is set in India: agents running a kirana shop, a truck fleet or a municipal office, dealing with people in Hindi, Hinglish and English.

I work with Dr. Dhruv Kumar at BITS Pilani, where I lead a team of student researchers building Karobaar, a suite of Indian business simulations used as reinforcement-learning environments. I am also interested in cultural bias in LLMs and in AI that is accessible to people the default design leaves out.

Projects

Karobaar: Indian businesses as RL environments for LLM agents Lead
2026 – present

A suite of simulated Indian businesses (kirana shop, truck fleet, municipal corporation, wedding planning, staffing agency, scrap dealer, hotel, cloud kitchen) in which an LLM agent runs the business over a long horizon and is scored against the simulator's ground truth. The shared core, bizgym, turns each business into an RL environment whose reward is the simulator's own score, with no LLM judge. We are training small open models (Qwen3.5-4B) on it now.

KiranaBench First author
2026

One model runs every job in a simulated Indian general store for 30 days (manager, cashier, helper and bookkeeper), learning the day only from its own staff's reports and dealing with customers and suppliers in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Demand comes from India's national household consumption survey. The best of four open-weight models earns an eighth of what a fixed-rule shopkeeper does, and every model writes worse to Hindi and Hinglish suppliers than to English ones.

BharatGUI: computer-use agents on Indic-script interfaces
2026 – present

Interactive, state-verified environments that measure how screenshot-based computer-use agents cope when an interface is fully rendered in an Indic script, starting with a government-service portal.

DiSCo: cultural preference bias in LLMs First author

A distribution-first framework for measuring how LLMs handle culturally specific norms and values, beyond language differences alone.

Agentic tool-calling in Indic languages

How reliably LLM agents use tools when instructions and context are in Indic languages rather than English. Ongoing.
