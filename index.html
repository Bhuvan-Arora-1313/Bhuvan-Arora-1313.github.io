Research

Karobaar: Indian business simulations as RL environments for LLM agents

Lead

2026 – present · Advisor: Dr. Dhruv Kumar, BITS Pilani

I lead a team of student researchers building simulated Indian businesses (kirana shop, truck fleet, municipal corporation, wedding planning, staffing agency, scrap dealer, hotel and cloud kitchen). In each one an LLM agent runs the business over a long horizon and is scored against the simulator's ground truth. We are turning these simulations into reinforcement-learning environments for training LLM agents.

First environment, KiranaBench: one model runs every job in a neighbourhood shop for 30 days, dealing with customers and suppliers in English, Hindi and Hinglish. Four open-weight models fall well short of a simple rule-based shopkeeper and write worse to Hindi and Hinglish suppliers than to English ones.

BharatGUI: Indic-script GUI environments for computer-use agents

2026 – present · Advisor: Dr. Dhruv Kumar

Interactive environments that test how screenshot-based computer-use agents cope when an interface is fully rendered in an Indic script. Ticket-booking and tax-filing environments are built; train and bus booking and government-scheme eligibility are in progress.
