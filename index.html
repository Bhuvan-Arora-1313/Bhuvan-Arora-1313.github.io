Karobaar: Indian business simulations as RL environments

2026 – present · Advisor: Dr. Dhruv Kumar, BITS Pilani

I lead a team of student researchers building simulated Indian businesses: a kirana shop, truck fleet, municipal corporation, wedding planner, staffing agency, scrap dealer, hotel and cloud kitchen. An LLM agent runs each business over a long horizon and is scored against the simulator's ground truth, and we are turning them into RL environments for training agents.

First environment, KiranaBench (first author, under review): one model runs every job in a neighbourhood shop for 30 days, with customers and suppliers in English, Hindi and Hinglish. The best of four models earns an eighth of what a simple scripted shopkeeper does, and two finish below a shop nobody manages.

BharatGUI: computer-use agents on Indian government websites

2026 – present · Advisor: Dr. Dhruv Kumar

Realistic, self-hosted replicas of Indian websites, in English and Hindi, used to test and train screenshot-based computer-use agents, with every task checked against the site's own database.

Built so far: a GST tax-filing portal (191 pages, 117 tasks) and an IRCTC-style railway-booking portal (109 tasks), each with an automatic grader and adjustable rendering quality. More portals are on the way.

first author

DiSCo: cultural preference bias in LLMs

2026 · Advisor: Dr. Dhruv Kumar · arXiv:2609.10253

A distribution-first framework for measuring how LLMs handle culturally specific norms and values, beyond language differences alone. We give models more and more cultural context, from none at all to per-option cultural facts, and measure how far their choices actually move.

A physical AI companion for older adults

Ongoing · Advisor: Dr. Siddharth Mehrotra

A voice assistant operated with physical buttons and knobs, as simple as an old radio, for older adults with limited digital literacy. The working prototype supports open-ended conversation, web search, and reminders and alarms; calling and voice notes with family are in progress.
